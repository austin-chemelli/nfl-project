# ADR 0023: Spreads and totals, market movement, swing factors, strong calls

Status: accepted 2026-11-07 (Austin; hub decisions D18–D21). Brainstorm and measurements: `.notes/plans/market-and-swings.md`. Tasks: M20. Builds on ADR 0013 (the market is the reference, never an input), ADR 0017 (one answer first), ADR 0018 (wave 2, the Student as the headline).

## Context
The site says who wins and how sure each approach is. Austin wants the prediction to read like a line too (a spread and a total next to the chance), to see how the market line moved over the week and why, to see what could swing a game (a questionable quarterback, injuries, a coaching change, weather, last week's result) with what-ifs, and to make the approaches' disagreements with the market visible, explained and tracked.

What exists: about 10 pre-kickoff odds snapshots per game per week across 9 books (`curated/lines`, since 2026-10-26), a mapped spread per approach (Φ⁻¹(p) × 13.5, the reference's own mapping) that the web only shows in a numbers table, divergence buckets and paper bets on the tracker, availability and quarterback features that follow the projected starter, narrative records with cited injury and coaching notes, and no totals model and no weather at lock. Measured 2026-11-07: a QB1 listed out moves the Student about 1.6 points of spread; the closing spread docks the same 1.6 for that coarse flag, and the home side covers 1.2 points worse when its QB1 is out (n 158, se about 1.1). The closing total misses actual totals by 10.5 points on average. No odds history exists before our own snapshots (the API's history is paid), so movement can't be backtested.

## Options
1. **Spread.** (a) Show the mapped spread. (b) Fit a margin regression per approach. (c) (a) now, (b) only if the backtest says the mapping's spread error is well above the market's.
2. **Total.** (a) Its own scored line from its first lock, in a side block. (b) Context only, unscored. (c) Nothing.
3. **Movement.** (a) Keep the current snapshot times. (b) Daily snapshots (Wed, Fri, Sat added): 9 more credits a week, about 140 a month against the 400 soft cap.
4. **Our own movement.** (a) Only the two locks. (b) A daily preview score of upcoming games, labelled and never graded.
5. **What-ifs and swing factors.** (a) The model's sensitivity, rescoring with the locked artifacts. (b) Change the approaches' injury weights now. (b) changes the public record mid-season.
6. **Explanations.** (a) Template sentences from the contributions. (b) The LLM preview (M6.3). (c) (a) now, (b) later behind the faithfulness check.
7. **Strong calls.** (a) The existing buckets. (b) A per-call ledger with reasons and outcomes.

## Decision
- **Spread 1(c):** the mapped spread shows next to every win chance (tile, card, game header). M20.9 measures its spread MAE against the closing spread's on 2009–2025 and records it here; a margin model only if that gap is more than a point.
- **Total 2(a):** a `student_total` line (ridge on both teams' efficiency, pace, explosiveness, special teams, venue and weather; walk-forward by season like the Student; the market total never an input, ADR 0013), scored by MAE and by its over/under side against the market total, shown in its own tracker block with weeks tracked and labelled as context. It never enters the headline scorecard or the README. An expected score follows from the Student's spread and the total.
- **Movement 3(b):** a daily pass (`nfl daily`: current-season ingest, odds snapshot, curate, features, site, publish) replaces the odds-snapshot job on Wed, Fri and Sat 20:05 ET; Thursday, Sunday and Monday keep their times. Per game the site carries the pre-kickoff consensus path, "first seen" (our first snapshot, never called the opener), the move since first seen and since the last lock. Snapshots after kickoff never count: the Sunday and Monday snapshots are in-play lines for games under way.
- **What changed in the window:** each move is annotated with what the data shows changed between the two snapshots: injury-report status transitions for starters, a depth-chart quarterback change, a coaching change dated to this week (the staff spells table), a forecast change, a lock, a cited narrative note. The words are "in the same window", never "because".
- **Our movement 4(b):** `marts/previews.parquet` (append-only) holds a preview score of every upcoming game from each daily pass, with the locked artifacts. The game page shows "today" beside "locked". Previews are never graded, never on the tracker, the retro, the recap or the README; a test enforces it, and the label says "preview, not locked".
- **What-ifs 5(a):** the quarterback what-if rescores with the alternative starter (the quarterback features follow the starter; `qb1_out` and `qb_change` flip) using the locked artifacts: "assumes Jackson plays: 71%; if Huntley: 54%". The lock stores the assumption with each strong call. Other swing factors are listed with the model's own sensitivity and, where M20.10 measured it, the market's typical reaction. No approach changes weight in this flight; if the study says the Student under-weights the quarterback gap, that is a wave-3 feature in the offseason.
- **Explanations 6(c):** template sentences from the contributions and the assumption: "The Student has BAL at 62% against the market's 52%. It leans on Baltimore's quarterback and offense and discounts the line's move toward Jacksonville. It assumes Lamar Jackson plays; if he sits, it reads 54%." No LLM in this flight (D21).
- **Strong calls 7(b):** at lock, every approach-game 10 or more points of win chance from the market becomes a row in `marts/strong_calls.parquet` (append-only): side, p, market p, spread and market spread, the three groups pulling our way, the assumption, the what-if, the sentence. Grading fills the result, the log loss against the market on that game and whether the closing line moved toward us. The tracker gets a Strong calls tab (live record per approach, the backtest's 10+ bucket for context, the list with outcomes); the game page a "Where we differ from the market" section; the retro a line naming the game's strong calls and whether they paid.
- **Weather at lock** (M5.7, revived as M20.10): `environment.temp_c`, `wind_kph`, `precip_mm` from nflverse game weather for the backtest and an Open-Meteo kickoff forecast for live games (a raw snapshot, `is_forecast`), domes null. They feed the totals model and the swing list only; the group registry doesn't change until wave 3, so no public line moves.

## Consequences
- About 33 Odds API credits a week; the guard and the soft cap stay. The daily pass runs the current-season ingest every evening (minutes, no credits).
- New append-only marts: `previews.parquet`, `strong_calls.parquet`; new model rows `student_total` in `predictions.parquet` (p_home null, total set). New site fields are additive (ADR 0015).
- Honesty: movement and strong-call records are live only, "weeks tracked" from 2026-10-26 and from the first lock that writes them; the what-if is our model's sensitivity, not the market's; the total is context and says so; the preview is not the record.
- Lock code changes (strong calls, the total line) mean a new image and the usual rule: never merge within 10 minutes of a lock.
- Risks: a questionable starter's status changes after the Saturday lock (the what-if is the answer, already on the page); a sparse consensus early in the week (a minimum of 3 books per point); the explanation sentence overclaiming (it names groups and an assumption, nothing causal).
