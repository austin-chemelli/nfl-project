# ADR 0033: The betting guide: rules from our own history, the math public, the card unlisted

Status: accepted 2026-11-20 (track betting-guide, model round 3; Austin approves with the PR). Extends ADR 0006 (paper ledger), ADR 0013 (the market is the reference), ADR 0029 (browser-only storage).

## Context
Austin wants to place small, manual, for-fun bets (Underdog, from Texas) and asked for a smarter way to choose them: singles or parlays, heavy favorites, coin flips, how much to stake, and spreads or totals only with strong evidence. The backtest shows no edge: the market's log loss is 0.6090 against the Student's 0.6243, and every paper ledger line loses money. Texas has no legal sportsbooks; federally regulated prediction markets operate there under dispute. Nothing in the project places, automates or integrates a bet.

## Decision
1. **A study on our own history decides the rules** (`src/nfl/betting/study.py`, `nfl betting study`, findings in `.notes/hub/plans/betting-study.md`). Moneylines 2006-2025 at the schedule's roughly closing prices; model chances from the walk-forward backfill (ADR 0005). Every rule search is walk-forward or split into halves, with the number of rules tried stated (186). The model is a parameter, so a new Student reruns everything; the stored study is rerun automatically when the backfill's model version changes, and weekly by `nfl weekly refresh`.
2. **The card's rules (guide v1)**, each from a finding, in `src/nfl/betting/rules.py` (shared by the study, which measures them, and the card, which applies them). Every side of every game, favorite or underdog, gets one view from its price band and the gap between the model's chance and the market's vig-free chance. A first draft (the model's pick only, big favorites "worth a look", a best-licensed-book price) was replaced before the merge at Austin's review: he wants to call his shot on a few games, underdogs included, and doesn't care which book is best.
   - Moneyline singles; never parlays (the cut compounds: −3.6% single, −15.6% at four legs).
   - *Avoid*: −120 to −185 (−6.2%, negative in both halves), +185 or longer (−7.0%), and the model 6+ points above the market (−0.5% on the wave-3 Student, −6.5% over 2021-2025; the model audit, ADR 0031: the Student's whole gap to the market is in its 6+ point disagreements, where the market knows the late news). All stay-aways together: −5.5%.
   - *Target* (second review, 2026-11-21: "there should always be a decent amount of targeted bets"): the week's five best sides the model backs (at or above the market, not an avoid), ranked by their history cell's return since 2006 (price band × where the model stands: below / same / lean 2-6 / big 6+), shrunk toward the average by 200 bets, graded A (≥ +2%), B (≥ −1%) or C. There is a list every week (4.3 a week on average; fewer only when the model backs fewer than five sides). Honest check, re-ranked each season on earlier seasons only (2011-2025): +1.5% a bet on 1,305 (+3.1% / +0.7% by half, −5.5% over 2021-2025), against −1.3% for everything else and −5.5% for the stay-aways; in sample +1.2%. Betting the model's pick in every game: −3.0%. The first two versions (big favorites "worth a look"; then fixed 2-6 point leans, which gave one bet some weeks and whose underdog pocket, +12.5% on the old Student, faded to +7.2% on the new one) were replaced before the merge.
   - *Targeted totals*: a spread or total must be ahead in both halves, have its 90% range above zero, be ahead over 2021-2025, and have a neighbouring version of the condition point the same way. Only the under in 15+ mph wind outdoors passes (+9.7% on 466 games, measured on recorded wind); the card shows it when the forecast reaches 15 mph. Nothing on spreads passes.
   - *The price needed*: the break-even price of the blended chance, logit(market) + w·(logit(model) − logit(market)), with w fitted on every earlier season (0.02 for 2026), in American odds and in exchange cents after a 0.07·p·(1−p) taker fee. The card shows the consensus price; it names no book.
   - *Stake*: 1% of the reader's bankroll a bet, flat. Kelly on the honest chance (each season's blend weight) stakes about 1.2-1.6% on a third to half of the picks and still loses 2-3% a season, like flat 1%; on the model's own chance it lost half the bankroll in 83-88% of simulated seasons. A fractional Kelly ledger variant (offseason backlog) is therefore not built: the study answers it.
3. **The paper ledger is untouched** (ADR 0006): the card is its own series, labelled with its rule version (`guide-v1`). Its record is derived from append-only inputs (live lock rows and odds snapshots, priced at the consensus snapshot at or before each lock), so a rerun reproduces it; a changed rule gets a new version, never a rewrite.
4. **Who sees what** (decision 3 of the round plan, recommendation (b)): the math is public, as a "Betting math" tab of the Method page ("How it works", `site/betting_math.json`), documentation rather than its own nav entry (Austin, 2026-11-20); the weekly page (the slate with targets, stay-aways and the strategy, plus the bet log) is **unlisted** (`/betting`, `/betting/week` redirects there; reachable by URL so Austin can use it on his phone, in no nav or public link, `noindex`). One switch per section in `web/src/lib/bettingVisibility.ts` (`public` | `unlisted` | `dev`, the last as `hiddenPages.ts`). The card's data (`site/betting_week.json`) sits in the public bucket like every site file.
5. **The bet log lives in the browser** (ADR 0029's pattern): game, side, price (American or cents), stake, fee, venue; graded against the season's results and our closing consensus (`closing` in `betting_week.json`) with closing-line value; exportable as CSV and JSON. Nothing is sent anywhere.
6. **Honest copy throughout**: no edge in 20 seasons, small samples, a fun test and not income, "not betting advice", the national helpline (1-800-MY-RESET) on every betting page. Texas facts are dated, sourced and "not legal advice"; no book is named on the card at all (the study's price-shopping table keeps to the four state-licensed US books in our feed), and no unlicensed or offshore book is ever named as a place to bet.

## Consequences
- The guide follows the model: when the Approaches track ships a new Student, `nfl betting study` (or the next `nfl site`, which notices the new version) refreshes every number; the pages print the model version they use.
- We can't show Underdog Predict's prices: the Odds API carries Kalshi and Polymarket US (`us_ex`), and `underdog` only in `us_dfs` (the pick'em board). Adding `us_ex` h2h to the snapshots would cost 1 credit per snapshot (about 11 a week); proposed, not switched on.
- The windy-under rule is measured on recorded wind; a forecast-based bet earns less. Revisit once a season of forecasts is stored (the weather family keeps every forecast it fetched).
- `nfl site` gains about 17 seconds when the study must be rerun (only on a new model version); `nfl weekly refresh` reruns it every Tuesday.
